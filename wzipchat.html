How it works

Three steps

Export a chat in WhatsAppOpen the chat, tap More, then Export chat, and choose Include media.
Open the ZIP in WZipChatTap import and pick the exported file.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app upload my chats?

No. The export is read and processed on your device. Your messages and media are not sent to any server of mine.

Does it need the internet?

Reading a chat does not. The free version connects to the internet to show ads, and Google Play uses it for the ad-removal purchase.

How do I remove the ads?

There is a one-time "Remove ads" purchase in the app. You pay once through Google Play, and you can restore it on a new device with "Restore purchase".

Which files can I open?

The ZIP file that WhatsApp creates with "Export chat". Choose "Include media" if you want photos, videos, audio and documents.

Is it affiliated with WhatsApp?

No. WZipChat is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
