WZipChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the WZipChat Android app (package com.wolfex161.wzipchat).

Your chats and files

When you import a WhatsApp export ZIP, the app reads and extracts it on your device. Your messages and media are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not run any servers that receive them. The app does not require an account.

The app asks for storage or media access only so it can open the ZIP file you choose and save files you ask it to save, such as a PDF export or downloaded media.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Your chat content is not shared with AdMob.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent form from Google before ads are requested. You can change your choice where the app offers it, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Removing ads

You can pay once to remove ads. The payment is handled by Google Play, and we do not receive your payment details. The app stores on your device whether the purchase was made.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
