WZipChat Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Welcome to WZipChat (package com.wolfex161.wzipchat). This policy explains how the app handles your data. Our core principle: your chat data is yours, and it never leaves your device.

Information We Process

WZipChat reads your chat exports entirely on your device. We do not collect, store or transmit your chats, messages or media to our servers or to any third party. We do not run servers that receive them, and the app does not require an account.

The free version of the app shows ads, which involves a limited set of data handled by Google. See "Ads and Third Parties" below. None of that data comes from your chats.

Data You Provide Access To

When you import a WhatsApp chat export (.zip file), the app accesses the following data solely to display it to you within the app:

  • Chat messages: text content, sender information and timestamps.
  • Media files: images, videos, audio files and documents included in the export.

This data is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded.

How We Use Your Data

The data from the ZIP file is used only to provide the app's core features:

  • Displaying your chat history in a readable, WhatsApp-like interface.
  • Letting you browse and preview media files from the chat.
  • Letting you search through your messages.
  • Saving media files or a PDF of the chat to your device when you ask for it.

Data Storage and Security

Data from your chat export is processed and stored in a temporary, sandboxed directory on your device. It exists only while you are using the app with an imported file. The temporary data is cleared when you close the app or import a new chat file. We do not create permanent copies of your data unless you explicitly use a feature such as "Download Media" or PDF export.

Ads and Third Parties

We do not share your chat data with anyone. The free version of the app does connect to the internet to show ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent form from Google before ads are requested. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Removing Ads (In-App Purchase)

You can pay once to remove ads. The payment is handled by Google Play, and we do not receive your payment details. The app stores on your device only whether the purchase was made, and it checks this with Google Play.

Permissions

WZipChat requests storage or media permissions on your device, only to let you select and read the WhatsApp chat export you want to view and to save files you ask it to save. The app does not access any other files or directories.

It also uses an internet connection for ads and for the purchase described above.

Usage Tracking

We do not add analytics or crash-reporting services to the app, and we do not monitor your activity inside WZipChat. The only tracking-related data is the advertising data that Google AdMob handles, as described above.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for people who are legally able to use WhatsApp.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
