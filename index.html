WZipChat

AndroidOfflineFree, optional ad removal

Open a WhatsApp chat export on your phone and read it like a normal chat. Browse photos, videos, audio and documents, search messages, and save the chat as a PDF. Everything happens on your device.

Download on Google Play Privacy policy
💬

Chat view

Messages with timestamps and senders in a familiar WhatsApp-style layout. Links are tappable.

🖼

Media browser

Images, videos, audio and documents sorted by type, with in-app preview.

🔍

Search

Find messages by text, sender or media name.

📄

PDF export

Save a chat as a PDF to keep or share.

🔒

Private by design

Your chats are read on your device and are never uploaded.

How it works

Three steps

Export a chat in WhatsAppOpen the chat, tap More, then Export chat, and choose Include media.
Open the ZIP in WZipChatTap import and pick the exported file.
Read, search and saveScroll through the chat, open media, and download what you need.
