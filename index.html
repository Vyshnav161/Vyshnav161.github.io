How WZipChat works

Three steps.

Export a chat in WhatsApp. Open the chat, tap More, then Export chat, and choose Include media.
Open the ZIP in WZipChat. Tap import and pick the exported file.
Read, search and save. Scroll through the chat, open media, and download what you need.
